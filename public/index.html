Frequently Asked Questions

Wholesale Bargaining Questions & Answers

What is BargainOS?
BargainOS is software that automates cart-level price negotiation for independent online wholesale merchants. It allows business buyers to propose a custom total price for their shopping cart, which is automatically accepted or rejected based on merchant-configured profit margin rules.
Who is it designed for?
BargainOS is specifically designed for independent B2B distributors and wholesalers selling standardized, repeatable bulk items—such as packaging boxes, industrial stretch wrap, sealing tape, and shipping materials—where buyers frequently request volume discounts.
How does automatic bargaining work?
When a buyer's cart subtotal reaches the merchant's required minimum threshold, the buyer can submit an offer. BargainOS evaluates the offer against the merchant's maximum allowable discount percentage. If the offer is at or above the minimum floor price, it is accepted immediately; if it is below the floor, it is rejected with clear feedback.
Can merchants set a minimum acceptable cart price?
Yes. Merchants configure two key rules: a minimum cart order value (e.g. $500.00) required before bargaining unlocks, and a maximum discount percentage (e.g. 15%) that sets the hard floor price below which counter-offers are rejected.
How does BargainOS connect to a custom-coded ecommerce website?
BargainOS connects via an embeddable frontend JavaScript widget (/widget.js) that uses a public Site ID to show the offer interface, paired with a server-to-server REST API (/api/v1/) authenticated with a cryptographically hashed API key. The merchant's backend provides authoritative cart details and verifies the offer at checkout.
Are Shopify and WooCommerce integrations available?
Custom-coded websites (Node.js, PHP, Python, Ruby, and headless frameworks) are fully supported today. Dedicated apps for Shopify and WooCommerce require specialized platform extension frameworks and are currently planned on our development roadmap.
What happens when a customer submits an offer below the minimum?
The offer is rejected immediately with an explanatory message showing the lowest acceptable cart total under the merchant's active rules, encouraging the buyer to adjust their counter-offer.
